Methods

Qualification

MEDDPICC
A qualification framework covering Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identified pain, Champion and Competition. It shows which deals are real and keeps the forecast honest.
BANT
Fast qualification on Budget, Authority, Need and Timeline. Useful for outbound and early-stage pipeline before deeper frameworks apply.

Selling methodology

Sandler
A buyer-centred selling system built on upfront contracts, honest pain discovery and mutual qualification, so neither side wastes time on a deal that won't close.
Challenger
Insight-led selling: bring the buyer a new view of their business, tailor it to each stakeholder and lead the conversation instead of following it.
SPIN Selling
Discovery through Situation, Problem, Implication and Need-payoff questions, so buyers put the value of solving the problem in their own words.

Deal execution

Command of the Message
A value-messaging framework that ties every conversation to the buyer's required capabilities, positive business outcomes and how you are different.
Mutual Action Planning
A shared, dated plan built with the buyer that maps every step to signature: stakeholders, evaluation, security, legal and procurement. Both sides own it, so deals stay on track and forecasts stay real.
