About

Led by Colin Constantine. Backed by experienced sellers.

Colin has spent more than eleven years on the selling side of enterprise software decisions at Salesforce, Sitecore, Algolia and WordPress VIP. He has led complex platform evaluations, run deal teams across pre-sales, engineering, product and customer success, and used MEDDPICC to keep forecasts honest. He is trained in Sandler, Challenger and SPIN, and has run outbound programmes and negotiated with procurement.

He has also built a company from scratch, so he knows what it's like to be the founder doing the selling. Coltasa exists to give growing B2B teams the enterprise sales discipline that usually arrives much later, and at a much greater cost.

Colin leads every engagement personally. Behind him is a bench of experienced enterprise sellers and sales leaders, brought in when your plan calls for more hands, specialist expertise or live deal support.
